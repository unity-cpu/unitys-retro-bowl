HOW TO DEPLOY ON VERCEL (fix for 404)
========================================

1. Extract this zip completely

2. Go to https://vercel.com/new

3. IMPORTANT: Upload the CONTENTS of the "retrobowl-vercel" folder
   (index.html must be at the ROOT of what you upload)

   Correct structure when uploading:
     index.html
     vercel.json
     package.json
     html5game/
       RetroBowl.js
       ...etc

   WRONG (causes 404):
     retrobowl-vercel/
       index.html
       html5game/

4. Or use CLI from inside the folder:
   cd retrobowl-vercel
   npx vercel --yes

5. After deploy, visit your URL
